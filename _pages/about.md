I’m Zikai (Kerry) Liu, an AI engineer in San Francisco. I build production agents and the runtime systems that make them dependable: tool execution, retrieval, sandboxed computation, structured outputs, citations, tracing, evaluation, and cost-aware routing.

At FurtherAI, I led the 0-to-1 development of a full-stack extraction agent now used in roughly 95% of customer workflows and tens of thousands of monthly runs. I also helped build runtime capabilities supporting more than one million monthly tool calls. Before that, I was the first AI/ML engineering hire at Articul8 AI, where I built a reusable ReAct Text-to-SQL service and worked on multimodal ingestion and retrieval infrastructure.

My current technical question is simple to state and difficult to solve: **how do we turn capable but probabilistic models into software we can trust?** I am especially interested in agent orchestration, durable state, evaluation, sandboxing, developer tools, and the boundary between model judgment and deterministic systems.

## Selected work

- [Agent Orchestration Is a Routing Problem, Not a Prompting Trick](/multi-agent-routing/) - a source-grounded study of mailboxes, worker sessions, dynamic workflows, browser/display ownership, and evidence-bearing joins.
- [The Agent Loop Explained: How Modern LLM Apps Orchestrate Tools](/agent-loop/) - a first-principles guide to tools, state, MCP, routing, delegation, tracing, and sandboxed coding agents.
- [DeepBrief](https://github.com/kerryzl77/deepbrief) - an automated learning pipeline for source discovery, grounded technical analysis, verification, print-quality reports, and feedback-driven prompt experiments.

I studied Industrial Engineering and Operations Research at UC Berkeley (MEng, 3.9/4.0) and Mathematics at the University of St Andrews (MA Hons, 3.8/4.0). Outside of work, I play tennis and travel whenever I can.
